# Applying the Discipline: optional mechanics

**Nothing in this file is mandatory.** [DISCIPLINE.md](DISCIPLINE.md) is complete without it.
This file exists for practitioners who *want* concrete mechanics; adopt any entry, adapt it,
or ignore it entirely.

> **Growth rule (v1):** this file holds at most 3 or 4 entries. A new entry is admitted only
> for a **proven requirement**, meaning a question actually asked in real usage. Mechanics
> born from imagined abuse (fake emergencies, gamed triggers, sabotage semantics) do not enter
> this file; that work belongs to audit and security, not to the discipline.

## 1. Necessity records

When a necessity opens a boundary (Principle 2), leave a record. Useful fields:

- **Boundary:** which line was opened.
- **Declared goal:** what the goal objectively compelled.
- **Alternatives exhausted:** each alternative inside the boundary that was tried or
  considered, and why it fails *against the goal*, not against your comfort.
- **One-off or structural:** a one-off sets no precedent; a structural necessity goes to the
  owner as a proposed boundary change.
- **Expiry or review date:** when this record stops being valid on its own.

**Write the record before you execute.** A record written after the fact cannot carry the
executing you back to the deciding you; it documents a justification, not a decision. The
timestamp is what separates the temporal split from granting yourself authority in hindsight.

**Recurrence threshold:** when the "same one-off" appears for the third time, treat it as
structural; stop recording exceptions and take a boundary change to its owner. In solo work,
the record is the voice channel between the deciding you and the executing you.

## 2. Containment and escalation

Principle 3's containment ruling, in practice. Decide these *in advance*, per context:

- **Who owns each hard and foundational boundary?** An escalation with no addressee is a
  shout into the void.
- **What travels with an escalation?** The verification (not the feeling), the impact, and
  the smallest counterproposal.
- **What happens when the owner is unreachable?** The default remains a safe stop;
  containment acts only within existing authority, in the narrowest safe form, and the
  decision still travels to the owner afterwards. For an AI agent, anything beyond a safe
  stop must be explicitly granted in advance, never inferred in the moment.

## 3. Proportional exhaustion

How deep must the alternative search go before a necessity counts as proven? **In proportion
to impact × reversibility.**

- Reversible and low impact: minutes of thought and one honest sentence are enough.
- Irreversible or high impact: a written search covering what was tried or considered, and
  why each alternative fails against the goal.

Both endless research and an instant "there is no other way" are escapes; the proportionality
rule is what makes exhaustion a practice rather than a slogan.
