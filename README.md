# Alp Discipline

*A decision discipline for the moment you meet a boundary.*

## What it is

The Alp Discipline has a single job: to help a human or an AI agent decide better at the moment
they meet a boundary. It is **not** a project management method, a craft guide, or a compliance
standard. It mandates no ceremonies, no tools, and no checklists, and it moves no decision
authority from owners to executors. It teaches the one thing a competent practitioner with good
intentions cannot derive alone, and leaves the rest to their judgment.

## Who it is for

Anyone, in any field, sector, or role: an engineer, a researcher, an operations coordinator, a
solo founder, or an AI agent. It requires no technical background.

## The four principles at a glance

*(This is an introductory summary. The single authoritative compressed surface is
[ESSENCE.md](ESSENCE.md).)*

1. **Draw the negative space first.** Name what you will *not* do, and prove that you
   understand the load-bearing boundaries by imagining their violation.
2. **Extension is granted to necessity, not to approach.** A boundary that blocks your
   approach is doing its job. Only exhausted alternatives prove a necessity, and you never
   grant yourself the extension.
3. **An honest stand on a route that leads to loss.** Carry verified evidence and the smallest
   counterproposal to the decision owner. Silent execution is never acceptable.
4. **The right altitude, a durable step.** Declare whether the work is a patch, a slice (a
   bounded, coherent change), a design, or constitution work. Every step advances durably, and
   papering over is legitimate at no altitude.

## For AI agents

Give your agent **ESSENCE.md, whole and unmodified.** ESSENCE is the agent's single canonical
Alp Discipline surface, and it operates within every higher priority constraint the agent
already has: system and safety policies, the law, and explicitly granted authority. The five
questions tell the agent *how to think*; the four floors tell it *what never to do*. Keep
exactly one compressed surface, because parallel variants drift apart.

## Implementations

The discipline stays single and simple; its implementations may be unlimited. Integrations,
agent prompt templates, schemas, and enterprise adoption models belong to separate
repositories, not to this one. This repository names no tool, no product, and no
organization, because the discipline must remain adaptable to any environment.
